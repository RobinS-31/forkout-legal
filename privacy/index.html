ForkOut

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

ForkOut is an app for sharing expenses between friends. It is in development and has not been released: it is not available on any app store, and nobody outside its development uses it.

Its full privacy policy will be published on this page before the first release. It will describe what data the app processes and why, how it is hosted, how advertising works in the app, and how to exercise your rights.

Advertising consent

ForkOut will be free and funded by ads served through Google AdMob. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before requesting any ad, and you can change your choice at any time from the app's settings.

Until the full policy is published, Google's own policies apply to the data its ad services process:

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
